Museum Guide Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

Museum Guide is made by Sen Lin. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use Museum Guide on iPhone.

What Museum Guide keeps

Museum Guide has no accounts. Your collections, saved artworks, notes and settings, such as the language and appearance you choose, are saved on your iPhone. They are never sent to me.

Recordings and images

Narration recordings and full-size images are downloaded from a media server hosted by Cloudflare, and saved on your iPhone so they work offline. These downloads contain nothing about you. Like any web request, they reveal your IP address to Cloudflare, which handles it under Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

iCloud sync

With Premium, Museum Guide can keep your collections and notes the same on all your devices. They are stored in your own private iCloud database, which Apple runs. I can't see what's in it. You can turn sync off at any time in the app's Settings, and you can delete the stored data from iCloud in the iOS Settings app.

Ads

Without Premium, Museum Guide shows a banner ad from Google AdMob at the bottom of each museum's home page.

Museum Guide doesn't ask to track you, so Google can't use your device's advertising identifier. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your IP address, and an approximate location worked out from it
  • information about your device, such as its model and system version
  • how ads are shown and whether you tap them
  • crash and performance information about the ads

Google handles this data under its own policies. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent in the UK, EEA and Switzerland. If you're in one of these places, Museum Guide shows Google's consent form before any ads load. You can change your choices later in the app's Settings, using Ad privacy choices.
  • Premium. With a Premium subscription, Museum Guide doesn't start Google's ad software at all, so Google gets no data through it.

Purchases

Premium is a subscription bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment, and I never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether your subscription is active. You can manage or cancel it in the iOS Settings app or from the app's Settings.

Children

Museum Guide is not aimed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect data about them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact
